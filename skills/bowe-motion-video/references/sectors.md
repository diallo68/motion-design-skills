# Sectors

Read the card of the main sector before writing the concept (SKILL.md, steps 2 and 3). Each card gives: the
clues that identify the sector, what makes its audience react, the proof to show, the structure (as shares of the
length), the visual genres that suit it, the look, the call to action and the traps.

For a mixed business, the main sector carries the film and the secondary one gets a single beat. The format
(story, feed, square, landscape) does not depend on the sector; the genre and the design do.

## Common rules
- Show the result for the person, not the list of features.
- The proof is a moment seen, not a sentence: a gesture, a before → after, a real number.
- Fit the client's country: language, currency (GNF, XOF, EUR…), payment methods (Orange Money, Wave, MTN MoMo,
  card), faces and places credible for its audience.
- Numbers, reviews, logos and claims: see "Honesty in ads" in SKILL.md. Health, money and results claims stay
  cautious.
- Two different sectors do not share the same genre by default. A series for one client uses a different genre for
  each video.
- No card fits (jobs, media, games, farming…): apply these common rules and say so when you announce the
  classification.

Genres name a template when one exists (`app-promo`, `website-launch`, `social-ad`, `iso-promo`, `collage`,
`map-story`, `parcel-3d`, `grid-montage`); the others are built from the moves. Moves are numbered as in
`references/moves.md`.

## 1. E-commerce
Online shop or marketplace, with or without an app.
- **Clues**: cart, prices, product pages, "Add to cart", delivery, returns, categories, promotions, sellers
  (marketplace).
- **Trigger**: wanting the product, then trust (delivery, payment, returns).
- **Proof to show**: the real product in close-up and moving (turning, texture, worn), the price, adding to the
  cart in one gesture, delivery tracking.
- **Structure**: hero product (0–20 %) · detail or use (20–40 %) · price + cart in one gesture (40–60 %) · delivery
  and local payment (60–80 %) · end card with the offer (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: `collage` (product photos and price tags), `parcel-3d` (the parcel opens, the products burst out),
  `grid-montage` (a grid cutting on the beat). Not a phone in the middle as the main genre.
- **Look**: a ground that sets the product off, the catalogue's colours, a fast rhythm on the music. Presets:
  `pastel-pop`, `bold-type`. Moves: 19 Punch-in, 13 Cards stack, 10 Counter, 26 A finger picks an option.
- **Call to action**: "Order", "See the collection", a real promo code.
- **Traps**: showing the app instead of the product, too many products in one beat, an unreadable price, an
  invented promotion, products that are not in the catalogue.

## 2. Education
Tutoring, online courses, languages.
- **Clues**: courses, lessons, quizzes, levels and grades, curriculum, teachers or trainers, certificates, progress.
- **Trigger**: being stuck or afraid of failing, then the pride of understanding or succeeding.
- **Proof to show**: a stuck learner who understands (a step-by-step explanation), a right answer validated, a
  progress bar moving, the teacher or tutor.
- **Structure**: the block (0–15 %) · the help in action (15–40 %) · the exercise solved (40–60 %) · the progress or
  the person behind it (60–80 %) · end card with the free trial (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: a quiz question full-frame that gets solved (the real interface), a notebook that fills, "a day in the
  learner's life" paced by a clock. Not a generic floating phone.
- **Look**: warm and clear, very readable type, a calm rhythm without dead time. Presets: `warm-editorial`,
  `clean-light`. Moves: 27 A chat writes itself, 9 Typing, 11 Bars and progress, 28 Progress fills.
- **Call to action**: "Try it free", "Find a teacher", "Take a quiz".
- **Traps**: listing subjects without a learner who progresses, clichés (mortarboard, pile of books) instead of the
  interface, promising grades.

## 3. Coaching, sport, wellbeing
- **Clues**: programmes, sessions, coach, goals, tracking, calories, class booking, transformation.
- **Trigger**: wanting to change without knowing where to start, then the feeling of progress.
- **Proof to show**: the body moving (a real session), tracking going up (streak, kilos, minutes), the coach
  correcting, a goal reached.
- **Structure**: the effort or goal (0–15 %) · the guided session (15–40 %) · progress tracking (40–65 %) · the
  community or the coach (65–80 %) · end card with the programme (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: `social-ad` on the tempo (hard cuts), full-frame counters, footage or photos of the real session cut
  on the beat.
- **Look**: energetic, strong contrast. Presets: `neon-lime`, `bold-type`. Moves: 23 Whip, 10 Counter, 11 Bars and
  progress, 19 Punch-in, 16 Big type takeover.
- **Call to action**: "Start the programme", "Book a trial session".
- **Traps**: still screens where the body should move, invented before/after, weight-loss promises with numbers.

## 4. Local service with booking
Salon, garage, clinic, craftsman, dry cleaner.
- **Clues**: "Book", time slots, opening hours, address, area served, service prices, reviews.
- **Trigger**: a concrete, urgent need, then peace of mind (it is booked, it is close).
- **Proof to show**: picking a slot in two gestures, the confirmation, the real place or craftsman, the result of
  the service.
- **Structure**: the need (0–15 %) · the service and its result (15–40 %) · booking in two gestures (40–65 %) · the
  place, hours, real reviews (65–80 %) · end card with the address and the button (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: the real place or craftsman in a photo with the booking over it, `map-story` for the area served, "a
  day" paced by the time slots.
- **Look**: concrete and local, real places and real people (with their agreement). Presets: `clean-light`,
  `warm-editorial`. Moves: 8 Cursor and press, 26 A finger picks an option, 21 Iris from the tap.
- **Call to action**: "Book", "Call", "Directions".
- **Traps**: a generic stock-photo setting, forgetting the address or area, invented reviews.

## 5. SaaS, business software
- **Clues**: "Free trial", dashboard, integrations, per-user pricing, "Book a demo", API, client companies.
- **Trigger**: a painful task or lost time, then control and time saved.
- **Proof to show**: a painful task done in one click in the real interface, a metric improving, an integration
  connecting.
- **Structure**: the pain (0–15 %) · the interface solving it (15–45 %) · the result in numbers or time saved
  (45–65 %) · integrations or real clients (65–80 %) · end card with the trial (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: `website-launch` (browser, enlarged interface), `iso-promo` (the workflow in isometric 3D), a
  before/after of the screen.
- **Look**: crisp, the interface enlarged and readable, little decoration. Presets: `midnight-glow`, `clean-light`.
  Moves: 19 Punch-in, 8 Cursor and press, 14 Browser launch, 10 Counter.
- **Call to action**: "Free trial", "Book a demo".
- **Traps**: a whole interface unreadable at that scale, jargon, client logos without permission.

## 6. Food
Restaurant, meal delivery, caterer, dark kitchen.
- **Clues**: menu, dishes, "Order", delivery, opening hours, address, table booking.
- **Trigger**: hunger and craving, right now.
- **Proof to show**: the dish in close-up (steam, texture, serving), ordering in one gesture, the delivery arriving
  or the dining room.
- **Structure**: hero dish (0–20 %) · preparation or appetising detail (20–40 %) · order in one gesture (40–60 %) ·
  delivery or atmosphere (60–80 %) · end card with the address and the offer (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: `grid-montage` of the real dishes, `collage` like a menu, photo close-ups with slow moves. Real photos
  of the dishes come before the interface.
- **Look**: warm colours, close-ups, a sensory rhythm. Presets: `warm-editorial`, `pastel-pop`. Moves: 19 Punch-in,
  26 A finger picks an option, 17 Marquee band.
- **Call to action**: "Order", "Book a table".
- **Traps**: stock food, an interface hiding the dish, dishes that are not on the menu.

## 7. Fintech, payments, mobile money
- **Clues**: account, transfer, balance, fees, card, licences, security, "Send money".
- **Trigger**: fear of fees, slowness or fraud, then trust.
- **Proof to show**: a transfer done in a few gestures, the instant confirmation, the fees shown, the signs of
  security.
- **Structure**: the situation, such as sending to family or paying (0–15 %) · the gesture (15–40 %) · confirmation
  and fees (40–60 %) · security and licences (60–80 %) · end card (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: money travelling on a map (`map-story`), the phone in 3D (`iso-promo`), big full-frame figures.
- **Look**: sober and reassuring, readable figures. Presets: `midnight-glow`, `clean-light`. Moves: 10 Counter,
  28 Progress fills, 9 Typing.
- **Call to action**: "Open an account", "Download".
- **Traps**: unverified rates or fees, promises of gains, a real number or real balance on screen, missing the legal
  notices the client asks for.

## 8. Health, telemedicine, pharmacy
- **Clues**: consultation, doctor, appointment, prescription, pharmacy, symptoms.
- **Trigger**: worry and waiting, then being taken care of quickly.
- **Proof to show**: booking the appointment, a real professional (with their agreement), the delay, nearness,
  confidentiality.
- **Structure**: the need (0–15 %) · being taken care of (15–45 %) · the professional (45–65 %) · confidentiality or
  nearness (65–80 %) · end card (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: the real site full-frame led by a finger, `map-story` (care near you), a night scene in 3D
  (`iso-promo`: what is open now), "a day at the practice" for the offer to professionals.
- **Look**: calm and clear, an unhurried rhythm. Soft transitions (24 Blur swap, 25 Dip cut) rather than whips.
  Presets: `clean-light`, `midnight-glow` for night. Moves: 26 A finger picks an option, 27 A chat writes itself,
  10 Counter.
- **Call to action**: "Book an appointment".
- **Traps**: any claim of cure, dramatised symptoms, patient data on screen.

## 9. Real estate
Sale, rental, property management.
- **Clues**: listings, price, floor area, neighbourhood, viewings, "Contact the agent".
- **Trigger**: picturing oneself in the place.
- **Proof to show**: the property in motion (a smooth walk-through), the neighbourhood, the price and area, booking
  a viewing.
- **Structure**: the property (0–25 %) · the key rooms (25–60 %) · neighbourhood and price (60–80 %) · end card with
  the contact (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: photos of the property with slow moves, `map-story` for the neighbourhood, an isometric 3D plan
  (`iso-promo`).
- **Look**: bright and unhurried, slow camera. Presets: `clean-light`, `warm-editorial`. Moves: 19 Punch-in (slow),
  13 Cards stack for the facts.
- **Call to action**: "Visit", "Contact".
- **Traps**: misleading retouched photos, a missing price, too many properties in one film.

## 10. Transport, delivery, mobility
- **Clues**: ride, courier, live tracking, fares, zones, ride-hailing, parcels.
- **Trigger**: being in a hurry, then knowing where the ride is.
- **Proof to show**: the order, tracking on the map, the arrival, the price announced in advance.
- **Structure**: the need (0–15 %) · the order (15–35 %) · tracking on the map (35–65 %) · the arrival (65–80 %) ·
  end card (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: `map-story` with the route drawing itself, `parcel-3d` for parcels, a city in 3D (`iso-promo`).
- **Look**: dynamic. Presets: `neon-lime`, `midnight-glow`. Moves: 10 Counter (minutes), 19 Punch-in on the map,
  20 Object becomes the world.
- **Call to action**: "Book a ride", "Send a parcel".
- **Traps**: promised delays that are not kept, a map of another city.

## 11. Events, ticketing
- **Clues**: date, venue, programme, tickets, artists or speakers, countdown.
- **Trigger**: wanting to be there and the fear of missing it.
- **Proof to show**: the date and venue very large, the atmosphere or the speakers, buying the ticket.
- **Structure**: the date and the name (0–15 %) · atmosphere or headliners (15–55 %) · the programme (55–75 %) · end
  card with tickets and countdown (75–100 %).
- **Genres**: full-frame kinetic type (`social-ad`), an animated poster, `grid-montage` of the speakers.
- **Look**: strong type, cuts on the tempo. Presets: `bold-type`, `neon-lime`. Moves: 16 Big type takeover,
  3 Ticker word, 10 Counter, 17 Marquee band.
- **Call to action**: "Book my seat".
- **Traps**: an unreadable date or venue, unconfirmed artists, pictures from another event.

## 12. Non-profit, NGO, fundraising
- **Clues**: mission, "Donate", volunteers, projects, impact, reports.
- **Trigger**: feeling useful, and seeing what the gift does.
- **Proof to show**: a real project and its concrete impact, what a precise amount pays for.
- **Structure**: the situation (0–20 %) · action in the field (20–55 %) · real impact in numbers (55–75 %) · end
  card with the donation (75–100 %).
- **Genres**: real photos from the field with text laid over, `map-story` of the projects, an amount that becomes a
  concrete object.
- **Look**: human, a dignified tone. Presets: `warm-editorial`, `clean-light`. Moves: 10 Counter, 20 Object becomes
  the world.
- **Call to action**: "Donate", "Volunteer".
- **Traps**: showing poverty in a degrading way, unsourced impact numbers, faces without consent (children above
  all).

## 13. Travel, hospitality
- **Clues**: rooms, stays, booking nights, activities, destination.
- **Trigger**: escape.
- **Proof to show**: the place in motion, the experience lived, the price per night, the booking.
- **Structure**: the destination (0–25 %) · the experience (25–60 %) · the room and the price (60–80 %) · end card
  with the booking (80–100 %).
- **Genres**: real photos in wide shots then details, `map-story` of the destination, a travel journal (`collage`).
- **Look**: bright and slow. Presets: `warm-editorial`, `pastel-pop`. Moves: 19 Punch-in (slow), 13 Cards stack.
- **Call to action**: "Book", "See availability".
- **Traps**: pictures that are not of the place, prices without conditions.
