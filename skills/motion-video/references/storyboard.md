# Storyboard

## Length by use
| Use | Length | Beats |
|---|---|---|
| Social ad (Reels, TikTok, Stories) | 6–15 s | hook, answer, 2–3 proofs, offer, CTA |
| App promo / launch | 12–25 s | intro, product, 3–4 features, breadth, end card |
| Website launch | 10–20 s | statement, browser, scroll + highlights, mobile, end card |
| Store preview (App Store/Play) | 15–30 s | features only, real UI, no end card with price |

## Rules that make it read
- **Hook in 1.5 s.** In a feed the first frame decides. Open on the viewer's problem in their words, a bold claim,
  or the product already moving — never on a logo alone.
- **One idea per beat**, 1.5–3 s each. A beat is one headline, one visual focus, one move.
- **Text is the soundtrack.** 6–8 words per beat, large, on screen long enough to read twice
  (about 0.35 s per word, never under 1.2 s).
- **Motivated cuts.** Change screen, headline and chip on the same beat; let the phone or the ground carry over
  so the video feels like one shot.
- **Rhythm.** Entrances 0.5–0.9 s, exits 0.3–0.5 s, overlaps of 0.1–0.2 s. Vary: a fast stagger, then a calm hold.
- **Safe areas.** In `story`, keep text out of the top 200 px and bottom 300 px (platform UI covers them).
- **End card 2.5–3 s**: name, one line, one action (URL, "Download", store badges).

## Template
| Time | On screen | Words | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.8 | the problem, big type | "Still splitting bills by hand?" | words one by one |
| 1.8–3.4 | product name, one line | "Meet Splitly" | zoom in |
| 3.4–7.2 | three cards | "Add a receipt / Split / Settle up" | cards stack |
| 7.2–8.2 | the offer | "Free for groups up to 8" | big number, counter |
| 8.2–10 | end card | "Download free" + badges | end card |

## Formats
Design for the primary format first (usually `story`), then adapt: `feed` moves blocks up and scales the device
to about 0.8; `square` to about 0.65; `landscape` puts text beside the device instead of above it.
